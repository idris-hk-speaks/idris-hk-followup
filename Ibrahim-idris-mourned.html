HON. IDRIS HK MOURNS FORMER KOGI GOVERNOR, ALHAJI IBRAHIM IDRIS

“A GREAT LOSS TO KOGI STATE AND NIGERIA” — IDRIS HK
Published: September 20, 2026 | IBAJI TV CHANNEL

Hon. Idris Hussein Kashim (Idris HK) has expressed deep sorrow and heartfelt condolences following the passing of former Governor of Kogi State, His Excellency Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, popularly known as “Ibro.”

Alhaji Ibrahim Idris reportedly passed away in the United Kingdom on Sunday, September 20, 2026, at the age of 77. He served as Governor of Kogi State from 2003 to January 2012, playing a significant role in the political history of the state.

Reacting to the sad development, Hon. Idris HK described the death of the former governor as a painful loss to his immediate family, friends, associates, the people of Kogi State and Nigeria at large.

According to him, the passing of Alhaji Ibrahim Idris is a moment of deep reflection, particularly given the late former governor's years of public service and his place in the political and developmental history of Kogi State.

“The news of the passing of His Excellency, Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, is deeply painful. We have lost a former governor, a father, a leader and a prominent son of Kogi State.

At this difficult moment, my thoughts and prayers are with the Idris family, his loved ones, friends, associates and everyone mourning this great loss.”

Hon. Idris HK also extended his condolences to the Government and people of Kogi State, praying that Almighty Allah would grant the deceased eternal rest and give his family the strength to bear the loss.

Alhaji Ibrahim Idris was elected Governor of Kogi State in 2003 on the platform of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP). Following a legal challenge to the 2007 election, he won a re-run election in 2008 and remained in office until January 2012.

During his tenure, his administration was associated with various projects and interventions in areas including infrastructure, education, healthcare and other sectors of public development.

Hon. Idris HK noted that the death of the former governor should also serve as a moment for reflection on leadership, public service and the responsibility of those entrusted with the affairs of the people.

Three Days of Mourning Declared

Meanwhile, Kogi State Governor, Alhaji Ahmed Usman Ododo, has declared a three-day period of mourning in honour of the late former governor.

The state government also announced that the national and Kogi State flags would be flown at half-mast during the mourning period and that Alhaji Ibrahim Idris would receive a state burial.

“May Almighty Allah forgive his shortcomings, accept his good deeds and grant him Al-Jannatul Firdaus.

May Allah also grant the Idris family and everyone mourning this painful loss the fortitude to bear it.

May the legacy of his years of service remain part of the history of Kogi State, and may his soul rest in perfect peace.”

IBAJI TV CHANNEL extends its condolences to the family, friends, associates and people of Kogi State over the loss of the former governor.
