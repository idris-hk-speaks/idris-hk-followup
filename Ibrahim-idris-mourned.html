HON. IDRIS HK MOURNS FORMER KOGI GOVERNOR, ALHAJI IBRAHIM IDRIS

“A GREAT LOSS TO KOGI STATE AND NIGERIA” — IDRIS HK
Published: September 20, 2026 | IBAJI TV CHANNEL

📢 Share This News

WhatsApp Facebook X

Hon. Idris Hussein Kashim (Idris HK) has expressed deep sorrow and heartfelt condolences following the passing of former Governor of Kogi State, His Excellency Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, popularly known as “Ibro.”

Alhaji Ibrahim Idris reportedly passed away in the United Kingdom on Sunday, September 20, 2026, at the age of 77.

Reacting to the sad development, Hon. Idris HK described the death of the former governor as a painful loss to his immediate family, friends, associates, the people of Kogi State and Nigeria at large.

According to him, the passing of Alhaji Ibrahim Idris is a moment of deep reflection, particularly given the late former governor's years of public service and his place in the political history of Kogi State.

“The news of the passing of His Excellency, Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, is deeply painful. We have lost a former governor, a father, a leader and a prominent son of Kogi State.

At this difficult moment, my thoughts and prayers are with the Idris family, his loved ones, friends, associates and everyone mourning this great loss.”

Hon. Idris HK also extended his condolences to the Government and people of Kogi State, praying that Almighty Allah would grant the deceased eternal rest and give his family the strength to bear the loss.

Alhaji Ibrahim Idris served as Governor of Kogi State from 2003 to January 2012.

Condolences to the Idris Family

The leadership and people of Kogi State continue to mourn the former governor, with condolences being extended to his family, friends, associates and people across the state.

“May Almighty Allah forgive his shortcomings, accept his good deeds and grant him Al-Jannatul Firdaus.

May Allah also grant the Idris family and everyone mourning this painful loss the fortitude to bear it.”

IBAJI TV CHANNEL extends its condolences to the family, friends, associates and people of Kogi State over the loss of the former governor.

🕊️ MAY ALMIGHTY ALLAH GRANT ALHAJI IBRAHIM IDRIS ETERNAL REST.
