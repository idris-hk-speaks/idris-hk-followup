IDRIS HK 2027 MANIFESTO

Hope • Service • Development for Kogi East

Our Vision

To build a prosperous, united and developed Kogi East where every community enjoys quality education, healthcare, infrastructure, security and economic opportunities.

Education

Provide scholarships, improve schools, support teachers, and promote quality education for children and youths across Kogi East.

Healthcare

Strengthen primary healthcare centres, organize medical outreach programmes, and improve access to affordable healthcare services.

Roads & Infrastructure

Support the construction and rehabilitation of roads, bridges, electricity and clean water projects in rural communities.

Youth & Women Empowerment

Create jobs, provide skills acquisition programmes, business grants, and empower women and young entrepreneurs.

Agriculture

Support farmers with improved seedlings, fertilizers, training, storage facilities and access to markets.

Transparency & Accountability

Promote honest leadership, accountability, and ensure that the voices of Kogi East people are represented.
