About Idris HK

Alhaji Idris Hussein Kashim (Idris HK) is a businessman, philanthropist and community leader committed to the development of Kogi East through education, healthcare, youth empowerment and grassroots development.

Our Focus

Education

Supporting students, schools and educational opportunities across Kogi East.

Healthcare

Improving access to quality healthcare services and community health support.

Youth Empowerment

Creating opportunities for young people through skills, jobs and empowerment programmes.

Community Development

Supporting roads, water projects, electricity and rural development initiatives.

Our Vision

To build a united, prosperous and empowered Kogi East where every community has access to development and opportunity.
