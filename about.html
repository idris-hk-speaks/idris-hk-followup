About Idris HK

Hon. Idris HK is a committed leader with a vision for a better Kogi East Senatorial District. His mission is to provide quality representation, promote good governance, youth empowerment, education, healthcare, infrastructure, agriculture, and economic development across the nine Local Government Areas of Kogi East.

The Idris HK 2027 campaign is built on the principles of service, accountability, transparency, peace, and sustainable development for every community in Kogi East.

Through this official website, citizens can register their PVC/VIN, locate polling units, upload polling unit election results, check verified results, receive campaign news, and participate in volunteer and ward coordination activities.

Vision

To build a united, peaceful, and prosperous Kogi East where every citizen has access to opportunities, quality public services, and effective representation.

Mission

To serve the people of Kogi East with integrity, fairness, and dedication while promoting development in every ward and polling unit across the district.
