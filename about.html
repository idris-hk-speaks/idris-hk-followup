About Idris HK

Alhaji Idris Hussein Kashim, popularly known as Idris HK, is a respected businessman, philanthropist, and public servant from Ajaka in Igalamela-Odolu Local Government Area of Kogi State, Nigeria.

He is widely recognized across Kogi East for his dedication to community development, youth empowerment, education support, healthcare initiatives, and humanitarian service.

Through his charitable activities, Idris HK has continued to impact lives by supporting schools, providing clean water projects, assisting widows and vulnerable families, and promoting grassroots development across communities.

His vision is to build a united, prosperous, and empowered Kogi East where every citizen has access to opportunities and quality public services.

Vision

To create a better future for Kogi East through quality leadership, sustainable development, education, healthcare, and economic empowerment.

Mission

To serve the people with integrity, empower youths and women, support community development, and promote accountability and inclusive governance.
